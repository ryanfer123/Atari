# Research

This directory separates evidence, assumptions, and validation work from product
documentation.

- `RESEARCH.md` records supporting research and its applicability to ATARI.
- `IMPLEMENTATION.md` records engineering hypotheses, planned experiments, and
  device benchmark results.
- `model-runtime.md` records the on-device model and runtime decision, upstream
  references, safety contract, and benchmark gate.

Product language must not turn behavioural fragmentation into a medical or
psychological diagnosis. Unvalidated thresholds and weights remain engineering
hypotheses until measured.
